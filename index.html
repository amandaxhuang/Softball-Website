Welcome!

I've created this website as a platform where you can find video-clips of my at-bats and defensive plays from games, as well as some cool school projects I've made over the years and academic achievements. I hope this website helps you get to know me as player and as a person outside of softball.

On a side note, I created this website completely from scratch - no website builder, just straight-up html and css code, with a bit of graphic designing done in Adobe Illustrator!

About Me

Hi, my name is Amanda and I'm a senior at Tesla STEM High School in Redmond, WA. My favorite classes in school are math, computer science, and physics, and I plan to major in computer science. I'm also very interested in aerospace engineering because I love airplanes and rockets, and hope to specialize in the computer science / software of aeronautics.

I am a c/o 2024 lefty slapper 2B and OF, and I play for WA Voodoo 18u Gold - Rhinehart out of Woodinville WA. Here is my SportsRecruit Profile.

Outside of school and softball, I love working on personal computer science related projects (like this website) and building lego models of objects that I like (NASA's Saturn V, NASA's Discovery Space Shuttle, BMW's M1000 motorcycle, etc. just to name a few). I also did competive robotics for 5 years, with my team going to the VEX World Championships in 2019, 2020, and 2021.

Some more about me!

I have a younger sister, Anna, who is one year younger than me. We love going on adventures (as we like to call them) together - beach trips, touring the areas where I have tournaments, and just hanging out.

I am also very proud of my job: Anna's personal taxi driver!

Amanda's Workshop

When I have the time, I also love woodworking. I took a class on woodworking in 7th grade where I learned how to use all the different types of power tools and how to select wood for projects. The art of crafting wood has been one of my favorite pastimes ever since. Some of my favorite projects are a long board I made for my sister for her 13th birthday and a working clock.

I also play the violin, and have been in school orchestra since 4th grade.
