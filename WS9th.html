Space Project


Class: Entrepreneurship

Description:

This is a two part research project. For part 1 (image), we had to research a current problem in the space industry in terms of accessibility and propose a solution. The problem I chose to research was the cost of transporting a resuable rocket back to base, leading to my proposed solution: rockets that self-land back in their launch towers. We then had to build a model of our proposed solution using arduinos to add moving parts.

Part 2 was a research project about anything related to space, and I decided to research the Apollo Guidance Computer on the Apollo 11 mission. The video is the documentary I made about the significance of that software.

Programming Language (Arduino): C#
