Here are some of my favorite school projects and academic achievements!

National History Day


Accomplishments:

- East Puget Sound Regional - 1st Place
- WA State Competition - 4th Place

Description:

NHD is a national level research competition. The 2019-2020 theme was "Breaking Barriers," where we had to choose a specific event or person in history that broke a barrier. I did my project on gender equality in the Olympics and focused on the 1928 Olympics and Betty Robinson, who won the first ever women's olympic event (track and field - 100 m).

For the "Lasting Impact" section of the project, I got the experience of a life-time: interviewing Ali Aguilar, my favorite softball player, about the coming 2020 Olympics.

Exploravision

Accomplishments:

- Honorable Mention

Description:

Exploravision is another national level competition I did in 8th grade. For the competition, pairs/groups of competitors research any science or medical topic and come up with a method to better address the issue. The final product is an 11-page essay, but for my science class, we also had to present our project to the class. My partner and I did our project on ACL tears; since the topic is so complex, our research focused on how to make better knee braces.

The competition is across Canada and the US, and sponsored by Toshiba.
