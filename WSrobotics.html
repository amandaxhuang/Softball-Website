Robotics... The Not Softball Thing

Outside of school and softball, I did competitive robotics for 5 years (6th to 10th grade). In 6th grade, my two closest friends and I started our team, competing in the Vex IQ competition. Then in 9th grade, we transitioned into VRC (Vex Robotics Competition).

Team: Universal Robotics 47774C
My Role: Robot Builder / Designer

Robotics Accomplishments

Over the years, we worked extremely hard to build our team into one of the best class of 2024 teams in the country. We went to the state championships all 5 years, and the VEX World Championships in 2019, 2020, and 2021.

Effort. Passion. Dedication

"Effort. Passion. Dedication" is my team's slogan, a phrase we live by. Through working hard, we've earned numerous awards and trophies over the years, including:

  • Dec 2018: Ranked #5 in World (Middle School Division)
  • Nov - Dec 2019: Ranked #1 in US, #4 in World (Middle School Division)
  • 2019 - 2020 Season: Ranked Top 5 in US (Middle School Division)
  • 2020 - 2021 Season: Ranked #7 in WA (True Freshman Team in HS Division)
  • 4x Tournament Champions
  • 5x Skills Champions
  • 3x Excellence Award
  • 4x Design Award (Engineering Notebook)
  • 5th place at 2018 State Championships
  • 3rd place at 2019 State Championships
  • 5th in division at 2019 World Championships
  • Top 20 finish at 2021 World Championships (True Freshman Team at HS Worlds)

It was one wild ride with my robotics team. And although I've loved every minute, it's time for me to focus on school (junior year at STEM coming up!) and softball. Although the trophies and awards were nice, what I will truly take away from robotics is the engineering and computer science experience that I gained, as well as the hardwork and dedication I put into something other than softball :)
